# Competitive and Market Moves

Use this reference for pricing, launches, market entry, response modeling, and war-gaming.

## Good fits

- pricing changes
- product launch sequencing
- entry into a new geography or segment
- reacting to a competitor move
- assessing a strategic partnership
- deciding whether to defend, attack, or wait

## Competitor profile template

For each competitor, capture:

- current position
- recent moves
- financial headroom
- speed of response
- likely strategic priority
- likely vulnerable point
- likely response to price, feature, and distribution moves

## War-gaming structure

1. Define the move in plain language.
2. Identify the 2-4 relevant competitors.
3. Predict the most likely response for each.
4. Predict the aggressive response if they feel threatened.
5. Predict the unexpected response if they choose a different battlefield.
6. Run one or two counter-move rounds.
7. Decide whether the strategy is robust or fragile.

## Market questions

- What is the customer's switching cost?
- What advantage does the incumbent already own?
- Is the market growing fast enough to absorb a fight?
- What can the competitor do faster than we can?
- What can we do that is hard for them to copy?

## Common strategic patterns

- price war
- feature match
- channel defense
- bundling
- segmentation
- partnership or acquisition response
- legal or regulatory pressure

## How to judge robustness

A move is more robust if:

- it still works when competitors react quickly
- it leaves room for a second move
- it creates learning even if it underperforms
- it avoids irreversible damage if the market turns

A move is fragile if:

- it depends on competitors staying still
- it requires perfect timing
- it assumes a single response path
- it creates a hard-to-reverse cost structure

## Output checklist

1. State the move in plain language.
2. State the competitor's likely interpretation.
3. Show the likely market reaction.
4. Show the no-regret elements.
5. Show the kill criteria.
6. Show what the second round of the game looks like.
