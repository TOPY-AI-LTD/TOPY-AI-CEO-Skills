# Example - New Market Entry

## Prompt

> Should we enter Japan this year or wait until next year?

## A strong answer should

- classify the decision as a Type 1 strategic bet
- identify the real question, not just the country choice
- compare at least 3 options plus do nothing
- highlight localization, channel, regulatory, and execution constraints
- run a quick bias check for FOMO, anchoring, and sunk cost
- show what evidence would justify a pilot versus a full launch
- end with a clear recommendation and review gate
