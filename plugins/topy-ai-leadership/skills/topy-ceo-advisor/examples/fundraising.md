# Example - Fundraising and Runway

## Prompt

> We need to raise capital in the next 90 days. How should we frame the round and what should we measure?

## A strong answer should

- treat this as a capital and operating-model decision
- define the minimum capital requirement and runway target
- identify the investor story and proof points
- separate leading indicators from vanity metrics
- show dilution, runway, and execution trade-offs
- include what would change the amount or timing of the raise
