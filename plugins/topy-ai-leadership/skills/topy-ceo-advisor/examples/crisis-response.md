# Example - CTO Departure Crisis

## Prompt

> Our CTO wants to leave for a competitor. What should we do in the next 2 hours, 24 hours, and 2 weeks?

## A strong answer should

- switch to crisis mode immediately
- lead with containment and access control
- separate facts from rumors
- define the communication order for board, leadership, and the broader team
- identify the single-point-of-failure risk in the technical org
- close with stabilization actions and a follow-up plan
