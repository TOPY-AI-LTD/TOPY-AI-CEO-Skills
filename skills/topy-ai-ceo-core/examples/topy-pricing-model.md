# Example - Pricing Credits vs Subscription

## Prompt

> We let users buy credits for business plan generation and subscribe to unlock unlimited projects and templates. Is the pricing model coherent?

## A strong answer should

- identify the decision as a capital and operating-model question
- separate consumption pricing from access pricing
- assess how credits, subscriptions, and limits interact
- point out which user behaviors the model rewards
- recommend whether to simplify, keep, or reprice the model
- show the metric or evidence that should drive the next change
